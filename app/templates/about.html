Text Summarization Tool is an open-source reading workspace that turns long English passages into concise summaries. Built for curious people, with a focus on clarity.

The language model

Hugging Face's sshleifer/distilbart-cnn-12-6 is a distilled BART model trained for English news summarization. It generates new sentences to capture the main ideas in your text.

Long inputs are split at sentence boundaries within the model's token budget. Chunk summaries are combined and, when needed, summarized again.

Explore the model

A resilient fallback

If model weights, memory, or connectivity are unavailable, the app switches to a lightweight extractive engine. It ranks sentences by meaningful word frequency and keeps selected sentences in their original order.

Every result shows the engine used. Three length options let you choose the amount of detail, whichever engine is available.

Your workspace

Try summarization without an account: guest inputs are processed without being stored in the database. Sign in to automatically save your original text and summary in your private history.

Other members cannot access your history. Authorized administrators can review saved content for workspace management. Delete individual summaries or clear your entire history whenever you choose.

Open by design

A Flask application factory, modular blueprints, SQLAlchemy models, validated forms, and a dedicated NLP service keep the project approachable and extensible.

Created by AliAdilQ. Released under the MIT license, with tests and setup documentation to help you make it your own.
